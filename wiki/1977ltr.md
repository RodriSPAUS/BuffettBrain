# 1977 Annual Letter Summary

*Report on Berkshire Hathaway's performance and philosophy as of March 14, 1978 (covering 1977 results)*

## 🔑 Key Themes

### Return on Equity Capital
- Explicit framing: "Most companies define 'record' earnings as a new high in earnings per share... we believe a more appropriate measure... to be return on equity capital."
- First formal articulation of ROE as primary performance metric
- **Source**: [[1977ltr.md#p1]]

### Tailwinds vs. Headwinds
- Foundational metaphor introduced: "importance of being in businesses where tailwinds prevail rather than headwinds"
- Contrasts textile (headwinds) vs. insurance (tailwinds)
- **Source**: [[1977ltr.md#p5]]

### Insurance Growth Strategy
- Early blueprint for insurance expansion: organic growth (National Indemnity), acquisitions (Home & Auto, Cypress), and new subsidiaries (Cornhusker, Lakeland, Texas United, etc.)
- Emphasis on "large gains in traditional liability areas" + new ventures
- **Source**: [[1977ltr.md#p5-6]]

### Business Acquisition Criteria
- First public statement of the four-part framework: (1) understandable business, (2) favorable long-term prospects, (3) honest/competent management, (4) attractive price
- Applied to both full acquisitions and stock purchases
- **Source**: [[1977ltr.md#p10]]

## 💬 Notable Quotes

> "We want the business to be (1) one that we can understand, (2) with favorable long-term prospects, (3) operated by honest and competent people, and (4) available at a very attractive price." — [[1977ltr.md#p10]]

> "It is comforting to be in a business where some mistakes can be made and yet a quite satisfactory overall performance can be achieved." — [[1977ltr.md#p5]]

> "In effect, we can obtain a better management result through non-control than control." — [[1977ltr.md#p11]]


## 📊 Investment Decisions

### Capital Cities Communications
- $10.9M investment; first major public equity purchase under new criteria
- Justification: "extraordinary properties and extraordinary management", "better management result through non-control"
- **Source**: [[1977ltr.md#p11]]

### GEICO Investment
- Large position in GEICO preferred and common stock
- Strategic rationale: "truly outstanding businesses sometimes sell... at very large discounts"
- **Source**: [[1977ltr.md#p10]]
