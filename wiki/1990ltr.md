# 1990 Annual Letter Summary

*Report on Berkshire Hathaway's performance and philosophy as of March 1, 1991 (covering 1990 results)*

## 🔑 Key Themes

### Shareholder-Designated Contributions Formalized
- Structural definition: "We suggest that new shareholders read the description of our shareholder-designated contributions program that appears on pages 54-55"
- Operational insight: "To participate in future programs, you must make sure your shares are registered in the name of the actual owner, not in the nominee name of a broker, bank or depository"
- **Source**: [[1990ltr.md#p0]]

### Managerial Integrity as Investment Criterion Deepened
- Structural insight: "Neither our operating managers nor officers of the parent company use Berkshire funds to make contributions to broad national programs or charitable activities of special personal interest to them"
- Behavioral contrast: "If your employees, including your CEO, wish to give to their alma maters or other institutions... we believe they should use their own money, not yours"
- **Source**: [[1990ltr.md#p0]]

### Annual Meeting Experience Codified
- Structural insight: "Attendance last year grew to a record 1,300, about a 100-fold increase from ten years ago"
- Behavioral insight: "The quality of our shareholders is reflected in the quality of the questions we get: We have never attended an annual meeting anywhere that features such a consistently high level of intelligent, owner-related questions"
- **Source**: [[1990ltr.md#p1]]

### Synergism at Berkshire Formalized
- Structural insight: "While there, stop at the See's Candy cart and see for yourself the dawn of synergism at Berkshire"
- Conceptual framework: "Synergism" as operational integration across subsidiaries (NFM, Borsheim's, See's)
- **Source**: [[1990ltr.md#p1]]

## 💬 Notable Quotes

## 📊 Investment Decisions

### Shareholder Engagement Framework
- Formalized principle: "An attachment to our proxy material explains how you can obtain the card you will need for admission to the meeting"
- Structural insight: "Because weekday parking can be tight around the Orpheum, we have lined up a number of nearby lots for our shareholders to use"
- **Source**: [[1990ltr.md#p1]]

### Retail Experience Integration Strategy
- Formalized principle: "We will have buses to take you to Nebraska Furniture Mart and Borsheim's after the meeting"
- Behavioral insight: "Those of you arriving early can visit the Furniture Mart any day of the week; it is open from 10 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. on Saturdays, and from noon to 5:30 p.m. on Sundays"
- **Source**: [[1990ltr.md#p1]]
