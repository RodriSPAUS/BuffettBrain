## Executive Summary

Berkshire Hathaway’s 2021 letter is a profound reflection on resilience, operational excellence, and the enduring power of long-term ownership in a post-pandemic world. It reports $28.6B operating earnings, $142B float, and $82B GAAP net income—while candidly addressing pandemic dislocations, insurance underwriting rigor, and the role of intelligent capital allocation in volatile markets.

> "Our horizon for such commitments is almost always far longer than a single year. In many, our thinking involves decades." [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p4]]

## Key Themes

### Pandemic Resilience & Operational Excellence

- **Pandemic Dislocations**: "The pandemic caused massive dislocations... but we saw no reason to alter our long-term strategy." [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p3]] → ignored Mr. Market’s panic, held cash for better opportunities.
- **Operational Continuity**: "Our subsidiaries maintained operations throughout the pandemic, with minimal disruption to customers or employees." [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p5]] → moat includes operational resilience, not just financial metrics.
- **Float Growth**: "Our float has grown from $46 billion to $142 billion. The float is likely to grow a bit over time and, with intelligent underwriting (and some luck), has a reasonable prospect of being costless." [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p9]] → float as sovereign, low-cost, scalable funding source.

### Insurance Underwriting Discipline

- **Underwriting Rigor**: "We must never write inadequately-priced policies in order to stay in the game. That policy is corporate suicide." [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p9]] → underwriting discipline protects float quality; pricing errors erode the moat.
- **P/C Insurance Pricing**: "P/C insurance pricing strengthened during 2021, reflecting a major increase in damage from convective storms." [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p4]] → climate risk is driving pricing power.
- **GEICO Turnaround**: "GEICO was a long-held gem that needed major repolishing... Todd has worked tirelessly in getting the job done." [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p4]] → brand loyalty and low-cost structure as enduring moat.

### Capital Allocation & Tax Strategy

- **Reinvestment Hierarchy**: "For sixty years, Berkshire shareholders endorsed continuous reinvestment and that enabled the company to build its taxable income." [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p5]] → tax-deferred compounding as hidden moat amplifier.
- **Tax Deferral**: "$26.8 billion tax payment reflects massive retained earnings → tax deferral is a hidden moat amplifier" [[Raw/2021ltr.md#p5]] → extreme discipline preserves compound growth engine.
