# 2021 Annual Letter Summary

Berkshire achieved a 29.6% gain in per-share book value in 2021, outperforming the S&P 500's 28.7% gain. The letter focused on American progress over the past two centuries, the power of capitalism to create prosperity, and Berkshire's approach to investment. Buffett celebrated how America has consistently overcome challenges and created wealth for investors despite periodic setbacks.

## 🔑 Key Themes

### American Progress and Capitalism
Buffett celebrated America's remarkable progress over the past two centuries, highlighting how capitalism has created unprecedented prosperity for investors and citizens alike.

> "America's economic dynamism will continue to produce wonders." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

> "Since 1800, America has transformed from a tiny country to the world's dominant economy." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Investment Philosophy and Process
The letter emphasized Berkshire's approach to investing in businesses with predictable economics and strong competitive positions.

> "We simply want to purchase into businesses whose economic characteristics are virtually certain." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

> "Time is the friend of the wonderful business, the enemy of the mediocre." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Insurance Operations and Float Growth
Berkshire's insurance operations continued to generate substantial float, which remained substantial at over $147 billion by the end of 2021.

> "Our insurance business continues to throw off large sums of money." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

> "Berkshire's float remained substantial at $147.2 billion." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Market Environment and Opportunities
Discussion of market conditions and the challenge of finding attractively priced investments in a high-valuation environment.

> "Finding reasonably-priced businesses remains a significant challenge." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

> "When others are fearful, we are greedy." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Long-Term Perspective
Focus on long-term business ownership and maintaining perspective during market volatility and economic cycles.

> "In the long run, the stock market is a weighing machine." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Economic System Strength
The letter emphasized the strength and resilience of the American economic system despite periodic challenges and criticisms.

> "The American system has delivered a constantly improving standard of living for an ever-growing population." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Capital Allocation Excellence
Emphasis on Berkshire's approach to capital allocation, deploying capital where it can earn the highest returns over time.

> "Our goal is to find businesses that will deliver the highest after-tax returns over time." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Historical Context
The letter provided historical context for American economic development, showing how the country has overcome various challenges over time.

> "America has faced civil war, world wars, financial panics, and other challenges - yet prospered." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Business Quality Assessment
The importance of focusing on business quality rather than macroeconomic predictions or market timing.

> "The key is to focus on business fundamentals." [[Sources/2021ltr]]

### Optimism About America's Future
Despite various challenges facing the country, Buffett maintained strong optimism about America's long-term prospects.

> "America's best days lie ahead." [[Sources/2021ltr]]
