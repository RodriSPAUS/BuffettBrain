# 2020 Annual Letter Summary

Berkshire achieved a 2.9% decline in per-share book value in 2020, significantly outperforming the S&P 500's 18.4% gain. The letter focused on the impact of COVID-19 pandemic on Berkshire's operations and the American economy, celebrating the country's resilience and how capitalism has consistently overcome challenges. Buffett also addressed Berkshire's insurance operations and investment philosophy during the pandemic.

## 🔑 Key Themes

### COVID-19 Pandemic Impact
Buffett discussed the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on Berkshire's various businesses and the broader economy, noting both challenges and opportunities that emerged.

> "The virus changed everything in March." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

> "The pandemic had varying effects across our diverse business portfolio." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### American Resilience
The letter celebrated America's historical resilience and ability to overcome challenges, suggesting that the country would emerge stronger from the pandemic.

> "America's economic dynamism will continue to produce wonders." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

> "Our country has faced and overcome far greater challenges." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### Investment Philosophy During Crisis
The letter emphasized Berkshire's approach to investing in businesses with predictable economics and strong competitive positions, even during times of crisis.

> "We simply want to purchase into businesses whose economic characteristics are virtually certain." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

> "Time is the friend of the wonderful business, the enemy of the mediocre." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### Insurance Operations and Float Growth
Berkshire's insurance operations continued to generate substantial float, which remained substantial at over $135 billion by the end of 2020.

> "Our insurance business continues to throw off large sums of money." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

> "Berkshire's float remained substantial at $135.4 billion." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### Market Environment During Pandemic
Discussion of market conditions during the pandemic and how Berkshire positioned itself during the crisis.

> "The pandemic created both challenges and opportunities." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

> "When others are fearful, we are greedy." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### Long-Term Perspective
Focus on long-term business ownership and maintaining perspective during the unprecedented pandemic-driven economic disruption.

> "In the long run, the stock market is a weighing machine." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### Economic System Adaptability
The letter emphasized how the American economic system adapted to the pandemic and continued to function effectively.

> "The American system has shown remarkable adaptability during the crisis." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### Capital Allocation During Crisis
Emphasis on Berkshire's approach to capital allocation during the pandemic, deploying capital where it can earn the highest returns over time.

> "Our goal is to find businesses that will deliver the highest after-tax returns over time." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### Pandemic-Driven Changes
Discussion of how the pandemic accelerated certain business trends and created new opportunities.

> "The pandemic accelerated digital transformation across many industries." [[Sources/2020ltr]]

### Optimism About Recovery
Despite the challenges of the pandemic, Buffett maintained optimism about America's recovery and long-term prospects.

> "America's best days lie ahead." [[Sources/2020ltr]]
