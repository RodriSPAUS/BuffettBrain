## 🔑 Key Themes

- **Textile Business Challenges**: The textile division had another poor year with persistent marketing and manufacturing issues. Management decided to stay despite poor returns due to employment commitments in New Bedford and Manchester, management's restructuring efforts, and potential for modest future profits.

- **Insurance Underwriting Success**: Premium volume grew from $22M in 1967 to $151M in 1977. Underwriting profits improved significantly due to prior rate increases, though margins were expected to decline in 1978 as rate increases slowed.

- **Learning from Mistakes**: Acknowledged major errors including the 1969 surety operation, Miami auto expansion in 1973, aviation "fronting" arrangement, and California Worker's Compensation issues. Highlighted the importance of being in businesses with tailwinds rather than headwinds.

## 💬 Notable Quotes

> "Most companies define 'record' earnings as a new high in earnings per share. Since businesses customarily add from year to year to their equity base, we find nothing particularly noteworthy in a management performance combining, say, a 10% increase in equity capital and a 5% increase in earnings per share." [[Sources/1977ltr#p2]]

> "It is comforting to be in a business where some mistakes can be made and yet a quite satisfactory overall performance can be achieved. In a sense, this is the opposite case from our textile business where even very good management probably can average only modest results." [[Sources/1977ltr#p4]]

> "We estimate that costs involved in the insurance areas in which we operate rise at close to 1% per month. Unless rates rise at a comparable 1% per month, underwriting profits must shrink." [[Sources/1977ltr#p5]]

## 📊 Investment Decisions

- **Textile Division Continuation**: Maintained operations despite poor returns due to social responsibility commitments and restructuring potential.

- **Insurance Expansion**: Acquired Cypress Insurance Company (Worker's Compensation insurer) at year-end 1977, expanding homestate operations to five companies with $23M in premium volume.

- **Reinsurance Growth**: George Young's department improved underwriting performance with a combined ratio of 107.1, showing a downward trend throughout the year.
