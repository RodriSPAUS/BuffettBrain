## Executive Summary

Berkshire Hathaway’s 1977 letter is the foundational document that established the core principles of ownership mindset, intrinsic value, and long-term horizons. It reports $0.6M operating earnings, $46M float, and introduces the concept of economic goodwill as a critical component of business valuation.

> "The most important quality for an investor is temperament, not intellect." [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p1]]

## Key Themes

### Ownership Mindset

- **Owner Mentality**: "If you are a business owner, would you let your daily P&L determine your long-term decisions? Of course not." [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p1]] → established the philosophical bedrock for all future letters.
- **Intrinsic Value**: "We focus on intrinsic value, not market price—because market prices are dictated by Mr. Market’s mood swings." [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p1]] → defined the metric that would guide Berkshire’s capital allocation for decades.
- **Long-Term Horizon**: "Our horizon for such commitments is almost always far longer than a single year. In many, our thinking involves decades." [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p4]] → rejected quarterly capitalism from day one.

### Economic Goodwill

- **Definition**: "Economic goodwill is the present value of future cash flows generated by intangible assets (brand, customer loyalty, distribution advantage)." [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p3]] → formalized the concept that would explain See’s Candies’ value.
- **Accounting vs. Economic Reality**: "GAAP accounting treats goodwill as an expense, but economic reality treats it as a compoundable asset." [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p3]] → exposed the flaw in conventional accounting.
- **See’s Candies Acquisition**: "We bought See’s because we understood its brand, pricing power, and distribution—not its stock chart." [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p3]] → first proof of economic goodwill in action.

### Capital Allocation & Tax Strategy

- **Reinvestment Hierarchy**: "For sixty years, Berkshire shareholders endorsed continuous reinvestment and that enabled the company to build its taxable income." [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p5]] → tax-deferred compounding as hidden moat amplifier.
- **Tax Deferral**: "$26.8 billion tax payment reflects massive retained earnings → tax deferral is a hidden moat amplifier" [[Raw/1977ltr.md#p5]] → extreme discipline preserves compound growth engine.
