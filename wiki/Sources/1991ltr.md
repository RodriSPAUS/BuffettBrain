# 1991 Annual Letter Summary

*Report on Berkshire Hathaway's performance and philosophy as of February 28, 1992 (covering 1991 results)*

## 🔑 Key Themes

### Intrinsic Value Growth Constraint Formalized
- Structural definition: "Charlie Munger, Berkshire's Vice Chairman, and I have set a goal of attaining a 15% average annual increase in Berkshire's intrinsic value"
- Quantitative insight: "If our growth in book value is to keep up with a 15% pace, we must earn $22 billion during the next decade"
- **Source**: [[1991ltr.md#p0]]

### Look-Through Earnings Framework Deepened
- Structural insight: "Look-through earnings consist of: (1) the operating earnings reported... plus; (2) the retained operating earnings of major investees... less; (3) an allowance for the tax that would be paid by Berkshire"
- Behavioral insight: "We also believe that investors can benefit by focusing on their own look-through earnings"
- **Source**: [[1991ltr.md#p1]]

### Media Franchise Economics Codified
- Structural analysis: "An economic franchise arises from a product or service that: (1) is needed or desired; (2) is thought by its customers to have no close substitute; and (3) is not subject to price regulation"
- Economic insight: "Media properties have begun to resemble businesses more than franchises in their economic behavior"
- **Source**: [[1991ltr.md#p2]]

### Managerial Excellence as Primary Value Driver Reinforced
- Structural insight: "The role that Charlie and I play in the success of our operating units can be illustrated by a story about George Mira... 'Now that's what I call coaching.'"
- Behavioral contrast: "Given the managerial stars we have at our operating units, Berkshire's performance is not affected if Charlie or I slip away from time to time"
- **Source**: [[1991ltr.md#p0]]

## 📊 Investment Decisions

### H. H. Brown Acquisition Strategy
- Formalized principle: "Much of my enthusiasm for this purchase came from Frank's willingness to continue as CEO"
- Structural insight: "Like most of our managers, he has no financial need to work but does so because he loves the game and likes to excel"
- **Source**: [[1991ltr.md#p3]]

### Super-Cat Insurance Competitive Advantage
- Formalized principle: "As long as apparently-adequate rates prevail, however, we will be a major participant in super-cat coverages"
- Behavioral insight: "Our ability to fulfill all its commitments under conditions of even extreme adversity is unquestioned"
- **Source**: [[1991ltr.md#p4]]
