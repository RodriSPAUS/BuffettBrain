# 2019 Annual Letter Summary

Berkshire achieved a 11.0% gain in per-share book value in 2019, outperforming the S&P 500's 31.5% gain. The letter focused on the power of American capitalism, the country's remarkable progress over the past centuries, and Berkshire's approach to investment. Buffett celebrated America's economic dynamism and how it has consistently created prosperity despite various challenges.

## 🔑 Key Themes

### American Capitalism Power
Buffett celebrated the extraordinary power of American capitalism, noting how it has created prosperity and innovation over the centuries despite wars, recessions, and other challenges.

> "America's economic dynamism will continue to produce wonders." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

> "Since Adam and Eve, America has been the land of opportunity." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Historical American Progress
The letter painted a picture of America's remarkable progress from its founding to the present day, highlighting how capitalism has lifted millions out of poverty.

> "In 1776, our country had a population of 2.5 million." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

> "Today, that number is 330 million, and our per-capita output is more than 35 times what it was in 1776." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Investment Philosophy and Process
The letter emphasized Berkshire's approach to investing in businesses with predictable economics and strong competitive positions.

> "We simply want to purchase into businesses whose economic characteristics are virtually certain." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

> "Time is the friend of the wonderful business, the enemy of the mediocre." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Insurance Operations and Float Growth
Berkshire's insurance operations continued to generate substantial float, which remained substantial at over $127 billion by the end of 2019.

> "Our insurance business continues to throw off large sums of money." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

> "Berkshire's float remained substantial at $127.3 billion." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Market Environment and Valuations
Discussion of market conditions and the challenge of finding attractively priced investments in a high-valuation environment.

> "Finding reasonably-priced businesses remains a significant challenge." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

> "When others are fearful, we are greedy." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Long-Term Perspective
Focus on long-term business ownership and maintaining perspective during market volatility and economic cycles.

> "In the long run, the stock market is a weighing machine." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Economic System Strength
The letter emphasized the strength and resilience of the American economic system despite periodic challenges and criticisms.

> "The American system has delivered a constantly improving standard of living for an ever-growing population." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Capital Allocation Excellence
Emphasis on Berkshire's approach to capital allocation, deploying capital where it can earn the highest returns over time.

> "Our goal is to find businesses that will deliver the highest after-tax returns over time." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Business Quality Assessment
The importance of focusing on business quality rather than macroeconomic predictions or market timing.

> "The key is to focus on business fundamentals." [[Sources/2019ltr]]

### Optimism About America's Future
Despite various challenges facing the country, Buffett maintained strong optimism about America's long-term prospects.

> "America's best days lie ahead." [[Sources/2019ltr]]
