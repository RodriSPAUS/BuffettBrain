## 🔑 Key Themes

- **Diversified Retailing Merger**: The merger added accounting complications including full consolidation of Blue Chip Stamps (58% owned) and restatement of prior years' financials as if the merger occurred at inception.

- **Textile Business Performance**: Earnings improved to $1.3M but still represented low returns on $17M capital employed, highlighting structural industry challenges with excess capacity and low margins.

- **Insurance Underwriting Excellence**: National Indemnity's Phil Liesche segment generated $11M underwriting profit on $90M premiums, described as "truly extraordinary" even against favorable industry conditions.

- **Worker's Compensation Results**: Cypress Insurance (managed by Milt Thornton) had outstanding results in its first year, while California operations were described as a "disaster" until Frank DeNardo's intervention.

- **Equity Investment Growth**: Insurance subsidiaries' common stock holdings grew from $39.3M (1975) to $216.5M market value (1978), with $112M in unrealized/realized gains over three years.

## 💬 Notable Quotes

> "The textile industry illustrates in textbook style how producers of relatively undifferentiated goods in capital intensive businesses must earn inadequate returns except under conditions of tight supply or real shortage." [[Sources/1978ltr#p8]]

> "The number one contributor to Berkshire's overall excellent results in 1978 was the segment of National Indemnity Company's insurance operation run by Phil Liesche." [[Sources/1978ltr#p10]]

> "SAFECO probably is the best run large property and casualty insurance company in the United States. Their underwriting abilities are simply superb, their loss reserving is conservative, and their investment policies make great sense." [[Sources/1978ltr#p15]]

## 📊 Investment Decisions

- **Merger with Diversified Retailing**: Acquired 100% ownership of Associated Retail Stores, Inc., a chain of 75 women's apparel stores.

- **Insurance Expansion**: Continued growth in insurance operations despite mixed homestate performance, with confidence in John Ringwalt's ability to correct issues.

- **Equity Investments**: Significant purchases of American Broadcasting Companies, GEICO, Kaiser Aluminum, Knight-Ridder, SAFECO, and The Washington Post Company.

- **Bank Divestiture Plan**: Required to divest Illinois National Bank by December 31, 1980, with spin-off to shareholders planned for second half of 1980.
