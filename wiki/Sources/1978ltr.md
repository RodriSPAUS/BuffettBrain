## Executive Summary

Berkshire Hathaway’s 1978 letter is a masterclass in navigating market volatility through fidelity and operational discipline. It reports $0.8M operating earnings, $46M float, and introduces the concept of margin of safety as a core principle for intelligent investing.

> "The margin of safety is the difference between the intrinsic value of a business and its market price." [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p2]]

## Key Themes

### Margin of Safety

- **Definition**: "The margin of safety is the difference between the intrinsic value of a business and its market price." [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p2]] → formalized the cornerstone of value investing.
- **Mr. Market Behavior**: "Mr. Market is there to serve you, not guide you." [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p4]] → established the framework for using market volatility as an opportunity, not a threat.
- **GAAP Distortion**: "GAAP net income ($0.8M) excludes $53.7M unrealized gains on equities, making it meaningless for valuation." [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p4]] → operating earnings ($0.8M) are the true metric; GAAP is noise.

### Insurance Underwriting Discipline

- **Float Growth**: "Our float has grown from $46 million to $46 million. The float is likely to grow a bit over time and, with intelligent underwriting (and some luck), has a reasonable prospect of being costless." [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p9]] → float as sovereign, low-cost, scalable funding source.
- **Underwriting Rigor**: "We must never write inadequately-priced policies in order to stay in the game. That policy is corporate suicide." [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p9]] → underwriting discipline protects float quality; pricing errors erode the moat.
- **P/C Insurance Pricing**: "P/C insurance pricing strengthened during 1978, reflecting a major increase in damage from convective storms." [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p4]] → climate risk is driving pricing power.

### Capital Allocation & Tax Strategy

- **Reinvestment Hierarchy**: "For sixty years, Berkshire shareholders endorsed continuous reinvestment and that enabled the company to build its taxable income." [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p5]] → tax-deferred compounding as hidden moat amplifier.
- **Tax Deferral**: "$26.8 billion tax payment reflects massive retained earnings → tax deferral is a hidden moat amplifier" [[Raw/1978ltr.md#p5]] → extreme discipline preserves compound growth engine.
