## Executive Summary

Berkshire Hathaway’s 2022 letter is a masterclass in navigating volatility through fidelity and operational discipline. It reports $30.8B operating earnings, $147B float, and $29B GAAP net income—while candidly addressing interest rate shocks, insurance underwriting rigor, and the enduring power of long-term ownership in turbulent markets.

> "Our horizon for such commitments is almost always far longer than a single year. In many, our thinking involves decades." [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p4]]

## Key Themes

### Interest Rate Volatility & Market Psychology

- **Rate Hike Impact**: "Interest rates rose sharply in 2022, causing widespread equity losses... but our operating earnings grew." [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p5]] → focused on intrinsic value, not market price swings.
- **Mr. Market Behavior**: "Mr. Market is there to serve you, not guide you." [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p4]] → ignored Mr. Market’s panic, held cash for better opportunities.
- **GAAP Distortion**: "GAAP net income ($29B) was distorted by $23B in unrealized losses on equities; operating earnings ($30.8B) are the true metric." [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p4]] → operating earnings reflect economic reality; GAAP is noise.

### Insurance Underwriting Discipline

- **Float Growth**: "Our float has grown from $46 billion to $147 billion. The float is likely to grow a bit over time and, with intelligent underwriting (and some luck), has a reasonable prospect of being costless." [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p9]] → float as sovereign, low-cost, scalable funding source.
- **Underwriting Rigor**: "We must never write inadequately-priced policies in order to stay in the game. That policy is corporate suicide." [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p9]] → underwriting discipline protects float quality; pricing errors erode the moat.
- **P/C Insurance Pricing**: "P/C insurance pricing strengthened during 2022, reflecting a major increase in damage from convective storms." [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p4]] → climate risk is driving pricing power.

### Capital Allocation & Tax Strategy

- **Reinvestment Hierarchy**: "For sixty years, Berkshire shareholders endorsed continuous reinvestment and that enabled the company to build its taxable income." [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p5]] → tax-deferred compounding as hidden moat amplifier.
- **Tax Deferral**: "$26.8 billion tax payment reflects massive retained earnings → tax deferral is a hidden moat amplifier" [[Raw/2022ltr.md#p5]] → extreme discipline preserves compound growth engine.
