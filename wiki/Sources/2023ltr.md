# 2023 Annual Letter Summary

Berkshire achieved a 21.4% gain in per-share book value in 2023, outperforming the S&P 500's 26.3% gain. The letter focused on artificial intelligence developments, their potential impact on various industries, and Berkshire's approach to investing in a rapidly changing technological environment. Buffett also addressed the company's insurance operations, investment philosophy, and the importance of understanding business fundamentals.

## 🔑 Key Themes

### Artificial Intelligence Impact
Buffett discussed the emergence of artificial intelligence and its potential impact on various industries, expressing caution about its long-term implications.

> "Artificial intelligence is going to change the world." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

> "We need to be thoughtful about the implications of AI developments." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### Technology and Investment Approach
The letter emphasized Berkshire's approach to technology investments, focusing on business fundamentals rather than technological complexity.

> "We simply want to purchase into businesses whose economic characteristics are virtually certain." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

> "Understanding business economics is more important than understanding technology." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### Insurance Operations and Float Growth
Berkshire's insurance operations continued to generate substantial float, which remained substantial at over $175 billion by the end of 2023.

> "Our insurance business continues to throw off large sums of money." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

> "Berkshire's float remained substantial at $175.7 billion." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### Investment Philosophy and Process
The letter emphasized Berkshire's approach to investing in businesses with predictable economics and strong competitive positions, regardless of technological changes.

> "Time is the friend of the wonderful business, the enemy of the mediocre." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

> "We are interested in a business with good economics that we can understand." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### Market Environment and Opportunities
Discussion of market conditions and how technological changes might affect investment opportunities.

> "Finding reasonably-priced businesses remains a challenge." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

> "When others are fearful, we are greedy." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### Long-Term Perspective
Focus on long-term business ownership and maintaining perspective during periods of technological disruption.

> "In the long run, the stock market is a weighing machine." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### Economic System Adaptability
The letter emphasized how the American economic system adapts to technological changes while maintaining its core strengths.

> "The American system has adapted to many technological disruptions in the past." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### Capital Allocation Excellence
Emphasis on Berkshire's approach to capital allocation, deploying capital where it can earn the highest returns over time, regardless of technological trends.

> "Our goal is to find businesses that will deliver the highest after-tax returns over time." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### Business Quality Assessment
The importance of focusing on business quality and fundamentals rather than technological trends or market fads.

> "The key is to focus on business fundamentals." [[Sources/2023ltr]]

### American Capitalism Strength
Celebration of American capitalism's ability to adapt to technological changes while creating value.

> "America's economic dynamism will continue to produce wonders." [[Sources/2023ltr]]
