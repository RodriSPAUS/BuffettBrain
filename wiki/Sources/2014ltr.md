## 📜 Source Summary

### Key Highlights

- **$18.3B net worth gain**, 8.3% per-share book value increase
- **Powerhouse Five** delivered **$12.4B pre-tax earnings**, up $1.6B YoY
- **BNSF service crisis**: $6B 2015 capital spend to restore industry-leading service
- **Insurance float grew to $83.9B**, 12th consecutive year of underwriting profit ($2.7B)
- **Intrinsic value gap widened**: Book value now a "crude but useful" proxy
- **Big Four ownership increased**: Coca-Cola (9.1%→9.2%), AmEx (14.2%→14.8%), Wells Fargo (9.2%→9.4%), IBM (6.3%→7.8%)

### Core Insights

#### Golden Anniversary Framework
> "Fifty years ago, today’s management took charge at Berkshire." [[p.2]]

- **Warren's and Charlie's independent commentaries** on past 50 years and next 50 years
- **Shared conviction**: "Bet on ever-rising U.S. prosperity" is near a sure thing
- **Historical perspective**: Real per-capita U.S. output sextupled in Buffett's lifetime

#### Powerhouse Five Economics
> "The $12 billion gain in annual earnings delivered by the five companies over the ten-year span has been accompanied by only minor dilution." [[p.3]]

| Company | 2014 Pre-Tax Earnings | Ownership Timeline |
|---------|------------------------|---------------------|
| BHE (MidAmerican) | $3.1B | Owned since 2000 (pre-acquisition) |
| BNSF | $3.9B | Acquired 2009 (6.1% share dilution) |

| Lubrizol | $1.1B | Acquired 2011 (all-cash) |

#### BNSF Service Crisis & Capital Discipline
> "We will spend $6 billion on plant and equipment in 2015... nearly 50% more than any other railroad has spent in a single year." [[p.3]]

- **Root cause**: Weather disruptions + capacity constraints, not mismanagement
- **Capital allocation discipline**: 26% of estimated revenues vs. industry average of 18%
- **Performance rebound**: Last 3 months showed material improvement

#### Insurance Float & Underwriting Economics
> "Our revolving float is both costless and long-enduring... the true value of this liability is dramatically less than the accounting liability." [[p.8]]

| Segment | 2014 Underwriting Profit | 2014 Float |
|---------|--------------------------|------------|
| BH Reinsurance | $606M | $42.5B |
| General Re | $277M | $19.3B |
| GEICO | $1,159M | $13.6B |
| Other Primary | $626M | $8.6B |
| **Total** | **$2.7B** | **$83.9B** |

#### Intrinsic Value Gap Widening
> "The gap between Berkshire’s intrinsic value and its book value has materially widened." [[p.2]]

- **Per-share investments**: $140,123 (+8.4% YoY)
- **Operating earnings**: $10,847/share (+19% YoY)
- **Float growth**: $41B (2002) → $84B (2014), creating massive unbooked value

#### Big Four Equity Strategy
> "Each increase of one-tenth of a percent in our ownership raises Berkshire’s portion of their annual earnings by $50 million." [[p.5]]

- **Ownership math**: 0.1% × $50M = $50M annual earnings impact across four companies
- **Unrealized gains**: $42B at yearend
- **Strategic flexibility**: "Appetite for either operating businesses or passive investments doubles our chances" [[p.6]]
