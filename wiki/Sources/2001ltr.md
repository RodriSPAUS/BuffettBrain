# 2001 Annual Letter Summary

Berkshire's 2001 letter discussed the consequences of the September 11th attacks and how they affected the insurance sector.

## 🔑 Key Themes

### September 11th Impact
The terrorist attacks significantly affected the insurance industry, causing substantial claims and reshaping risk assessment practices.

### Insurance Market Changes
Following the attacks, there were notable shifts in the reinsurance market as companies reassessed their exposure to terrorism-related risks.
