## 🔑 Key Themes

- **Accounting Policy Change**: New requirement to carry equity securities at market value (vs. lower of cost or market), significantly increasing yearend net worth with appropriate tax liability.

- **Inflation Impact Analysis**: Introduced the "investor's misery index" concept - combination of inflation rate plus tax rate on capital gains that can erode real purchasing power even with strong nominal returns.

- **Textile Business Mistake**: Acknowledged the Waumbec Mills acquisition as a "mistake" despite bargain purchase price, concluding that "turnarounds seldom turn" and talent is better employed in good businesses at fair prices.

- **Insurance Industry Performance**: Industry combined ratio rose from 97.4% to 100.7%, throwing industry into underwriting loss position, while Berkshire improved its relative performance (ratio decreased from 98.2% to 97.1%).

- **Bond Investment Philosophy**: Critical analysis of long-term fixed-interest bonds in inflationary environment, concluding they may become obsolete instruments; shifted focus to convertible bonds with shorter effective lives.

## 💬 Notable Quotes

> "A few years ago, a business whose per-share net worth compounded at 20% annually would have guaranteed its owners a highly successful real investment return. Now such an outcome seems less certain." [[Sources/1979ltr]]

> "The combination - the inflation rate plus the percentage of capital that must be paid by the owner to transfer into his own pocket the annual earnings achieved by the business... can be thought of as an 'investor's misery index'." [[Sources/1979ltr]]

> "Both our operating and investment experience cause us to conclude that 'turnarounds' seldom turn, and that the same energies and talent are much better employed in a good business purchased at a fair price than in a poor business purchased at a bargain price." [[Sources/1979ltr]]

## 📊 Investment Decisions

- **Accounting Policy Implementation**: Adopted new accounting standard for equity securities valuation at market value.

- **Bond Portfolio Restructuring**: Ceased net purchases of straight long-term bonds, shifted focus to convertible bonds with shorter effective lives.

- **Bank Divestiture Planning**: Continued investigation of bank sale options (80-100% stock sale) after Federal Reserve restrictions prevented spin-off with shared management.

- **Insurance Strategy Adjustment**: Recognized need to accept higher industry combined ratios (forecast 105+ over next five years) and focused on maintaining competitive advantage through superior management.
