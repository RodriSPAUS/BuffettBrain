# ManagementQuality

*Buffett's criteria for evaluating the leadership of businesses Berkshire owns or considers acquiring.*

## 📚 Definition

ManagementQuality is assessed not by credentials or pedigree, but by *fidelity*, *rationality*, *candor*, and *resistance to institutional imperative*. Buffett prioritizes managers who act as owners — aligning their interests with shareholders through compensation tied to economic performance.

> "A decent batting average in personnel decisions is all that can be hoped for. The cardinal sin is delaying the correction of mistakes." — [[2024ltr.md#p1]]

## ✅ Core Criteria

| Criterion | Description | Evidence from Letters |
|-----------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Fidelity** | Unwavering honesty and alignment with shareholder interests | Pete Liegl's $100k base + 10% bonus on incremental earnings — [[2024ltr.md#p2-3]] |
| **Rationality** | Decision-making grounded in economic reality, not ego or convention | Avoiding "institutional imperative" — [[1993.md]] |
| **Candor** | Direct, transparent communication — even about mistakes | Using "mistake" or "error" 16 times (2019–2023) — [[2024ltr.md#p1]] |
| **CompensationRestraint** | Pay structures that reward value creation, not tenure or title | Japanese managers' conservative pay vs. U.S. peers — [[2024ltr.md#p10]] |
| **SchoolAgnoticism** | Elite education is irrelevant; innate talent and integrity matter more | "I never look at where a candidate has gone to school. Never!" — [[2024ltr.md#p3]] |
