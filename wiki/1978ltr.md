# 1978 Annual Letter Summary

*Report on Berkshire Hathaway's performance and philosophy as of March 26, 1979 (covering 1978 results)*

## 🔑 Key Themes

### Accounting Transparency & Segment Reporting
- Explicit framing: "grouping of Balance Sheet and Earnings items... tends to obscure economic reality more than illuminate it"
- First formal articulation of the need for segmented financial reporting to reveal true economics
- **Source**: [[1978ltr.md#p1]]

### Insurance Cycle Awareness
- Formalized understanding of insurance underwriting cycles: "The pendulum now is beginning to swing the other way" (1978 peak → 1979 downturn)
- Quantitative modeling: "costs involved in the insurance areas... rise at close to 1% per month"
- **Source**: [[1978ltr.md#p6-7]]

### Concentrated Equity Strategy
- Explicit policy: "We try to avoid buying a little of this or that when we are only lukewarm... When we are convinced as to attractiveness, we believe in buying worthwhile amounts."
- SAFECO case study: purchasing "best company in the business" at <100% book value while peers pay >100%
- **Source**: [[1978ltr.md#p10-11]]

### Managerial Longevity & Proprietary Attitude
- Triad of managers: Gene Abegg (81), Ben Rosner (75), Louie Vincenti (73)
- Core insight: "managers who enjoy coming to work each morning and, once there, instinctively and unerringly think like owners"
- **Source**: [[1978ltr.md#p14]]

## 💬 Notable Quotes

> "It is not easy to buy a good insurance business, but our experience has been that it is easier to buy one than create one." — [[1978ltr.md#p8]]

> "We are not concerned with whether the market quickly revalues upward securities that we believe are selling at bargain prices. In fact, we prefer just the opposite..." — [[1978ltr.md#p10]]

> "managers who enjoy coming to work each morning and, once there, instinctively and unerringly think like owners" — [[1978ltr.md#p14]]


## 📊 Investment Decisions

### SAFECO Corporation
- 953,750 shares purchased at <100% book value
- Strategic rationale: "best run large property and casualty insurance company in the United States", "superb underwriting abilities", "conservative loss reserving"
- **Source**: [[1978ltr.md#p11]]

### GEICO Investment Expansion
- Increased position in GEICO common and preferred stock
- Reinforced thesis: "truly outstanding businesses sometimes sell... at very large discounts"
- **Source**: [[1978ltr.md#p10]]
