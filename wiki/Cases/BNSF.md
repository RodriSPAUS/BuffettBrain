## Why it belongs here

BNSF is the other half of the regulated, capital-hungry pair that Buffett splits
out from the rest of Berkshire — see [[Cases/BerkshireHathawayEnergy]] for the
argument that applies to both:

> "Regulated, Capital-Intensive Businesses We have two major operations, BNSF and
> Berkshire Hathaway Energy ("BHE"), that share important characteristics
> distinguishing them from our other businesses." [[Sources/2014ltr]]

> "A century hence, BNSF and Berkshire Hathaway Energy will still be playing vital
> roles in our economy." [[Sources/2014ltr]]

## The advantage claimed

> "Both of us are enthusiastic about BNSF's future because railroads have major
> cost and environmental advantages over trucking, their main competitor."
> [[Sources/2010ltr]]

> "Last year BNSF moved each ton of freight it carried a record 500 miles on a
> single gallon of diesel fuel." [[Sources/2010ltr]]

That is a [[Concepts/Moat]] of the 1982 kind: a wide and sustainable cost
advantage in what is otherwise a commodity business.
