# 1987 Annual Letter Summary

*Report on Berkshire Hathaway's performance and philosophy as of February 26, 1988 (covering 1987 results)*

## 🔑 Key Themes

### Managerial Excellence Quantified
- Formalized benchmark: "If these seven business units had operated as a single company, their 1987 after-tax earnings would have been approximately $100 million - a return of about 57% on equity capital"
- Structural insight: "The managers of the units should be judged by the returns they achieve on the underlying assets; what we pay for a business does not affect the amount of capital its manager has to work with"
- **Source**: [[1987ltr.md#p0]]

### Business Franchise Stability Framework
- Core principle: "the best business returns are usually achieved by companies that are doing something quite similar today to what they were doing five or ten years ago"
- Structural constraint: "economic terrain that is forever shifting violently is ground on which it is difficult to build a fortress-like business franchise"
- **Source**: [[1987ltr.md#p1]]

### Insurance Industry Commodity Economics Deepened
- Structural analysis: "hundreds of competitors, ease of entry, and a product that cannot be differentiated in any meaningful way"
- Strategic response: "differentiate our product by our financial strength" and "total indifference to volume"
- **Source**: [[1987ltr.md#p3]]

### Mr. Market Allegory Codification
- Formalized investment framework: "imagine market quotations as coming from a remarkably accommodating fellow named Mr. Market who is your partner in a private business"
- Behavioral principle: "Mr. Market is there to serve you, not to guide you. It is his pocketbook, not his wisdom, that you will find useful"
- **Source**: [[1987ltr.md#p4]]

## 📊 Investment Decisions

### Permanent Holdings Philosophy
- Formalized principle: "we approach the transaction as if we were buying into a private business" with no time or price for sale
- Structural constraint: "our economic fate will be determined by the economic fate of the business we own, whether our ownership is partial or total"
- **Source**: [[1987ltr.md#p4]]
