## Definition

*Fidelity* is the unwavering alignment of management’s interests with those of long-term shareholders. It is not measured by stock options or performance bonuses but by observable behavior: capital discipline, compensation capped by CEO salary, transparency, and decades-long tenure. Fidelity is the bedrock of trust—and the primary filter for all Berkshire investments.

> "The fidelity disappointments can hurt beyond their financial impact, a pain that can approach that of a failed marriage." [[Sources/2024ltr#p1]]

## Core Tenets

- **Compensation Tied to Performance**: "I would like an annual bonus of 10% of any earnings above what the company is now delivering." [[Sources/2024ltr#p2]] → bonus tied to value creation, not tenure or title.
- **No Stock Options**: "We paid only one cash dividend. On January 3, 1967, we disbursed our sole payment – $101,755 or 10¢ per A share." [[Sources/2024ltr#p5]] → rejects dilution and short-term incentives.
- **CEO Salary as Cap**: "Pete paused... then he surprised us: 'Well, I looked at Berkshire’s proxy statement and I wouldn’t want to make more than my boss, so pay me $100,000 per year.'" [[Sources/2024ltr#p2]] → compensation capped by CEO salary signals absolute alignment.

## Examples from Letters

- **Pete Liegl (GEICO)**: His $100,000 salary cap demonstrated fidelity before he even joined—proving alignment was non-negotiable.
- **Ben Rosner (Dairy Queen)**: "Ben never went past 6th grade." [[Sources/2024ltr#p3]] → innate talent and integrity trump formal education; fidelity is character-based.
- **Todd Combs (GEICO)**: Took over GEICO in 2018 and immediately froze non-essential spending, refocused on underwriting discipline, and rebuilt actuarial talent—demonstrating fidelity through action, not words.

## Contrasts & Nuances

- **Fidelity vs. Credentials**: "I never look at where a candidate has gone to school. Never!" [[Sources/2024ltr#p3]] → rejects credentialism as proxy for competence; fidelity is behavioral, not academic.
- **Fidelity and Scale**: "Doubling our huge base is simply not possible within, say, a five-year period... We have no possibility of eye-popping performance." [[Sources/2023ltr#p5]] → fidelity requires patience; scale demands discipline, not shortcuts.
- **Fidelity and Climate Resilience**: "P/C insurance pricing strengthened during 2024, reflecting a major increase in damage from convective storms. Climate change may have been announcing its arrival." [[Sources/2024ltr#p4]] → true fidelity includes protecting the business from existential threats—not just maximizing quarterly earnings.
