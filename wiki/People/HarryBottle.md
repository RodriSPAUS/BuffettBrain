# Harry Bottle

The outside operator brought in to run [[Cases/DempsterMill]] once it became
clear the existing management could not fix it — and the partnership's first
proof that a control situation could be actively worked, not just bought
cheap and waited out.

## The hire

Recommended by a friend "whose inclination is not toward enthusiastic
descriptions," Bottle was installed within a week of first contact:

> "On April 17, 1962 I met Harry in Los Angeles, presented a deal which provided for rewards to him based upon our objectives being met, and on April 23rd he was sitting in the president's chair in Beatrice." [[Sources/1963ltr]]

## The turnaround

Buffett's verdict, unqualified:

> "Harry is unquestionably the man of the year." [[Sources/1963ltr]]

The record, told through comparative balance sheets from November 1961 to
July 1963, credits Bottle with nine specific fixes — inventory cut from over
$4 million to under $1 million, administrative expense cut in half, five
unprofitable branches closed, factory overhead per labor-hour reduced — all
converting a stagnant manufacturer into a source of cash for the securities
portfolio. [[Concepts/MarginOfSafety]]

## What he wanted, and what he became

Buffett notes Bottle's incentive structure approvingly, on the same terms he
applies to himself:

> "Harry works the same way I do -- he likes big carrots." [[Sources/1964ltr]]

By late 1963, Bottle had become a limited partner in Buffett Partnership,
Ltd. himself:

> "Harry, incidentally, has made an advance payment toward becoming a limited partner in 1964-- we consider this the beginning, not the end." [[Sources/1963ltr]]
