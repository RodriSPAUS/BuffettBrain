# Tom Murphy

CEO of Capital Cities Communications, which acquired ABC in 1985 with Berkshire
as its financing partner.

## Why Buffett singles him out

In the 1981 letter's dissection of acquisition behaviour, Murphy is the only
manager named in both of the two categories of acquirer whose records Buffett
considers earned — those who bought businesses adapted to inflation, and the
managerial superstars able to make an ordinary business better. Buffett calls him
a twofer, "whose acquisition efforts have been properly focused in Category 1 and
whose operating talents also make him a leader of Category 2" [[Sources/1981ltr]].

The same letter records what such managers actually did with capital in that
period: "these champs have made very few deals in recent years, and often have
found repurchase of their own shares to be the most sensible employment of
corporate capital" [[Sources/1981ltr]].

## Capital Cities/ABC

Berkshire bought 3 million shares at $172.50 in 1985 and handed its votes to
Murphy — an arrangement Buffett says he and [[People/CharlieMunger]] initiated
rather than Murphy: "for an extended period Tom, as CEO (or Dan, should he be
CEO) votes our stock. This arrangement was initiated by Charlie and me, not by
Tom." [[Sources/1985ltr]]
