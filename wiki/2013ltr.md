## 📜 Source Summary

### Key Highlights

- **$34.2B net worth gain** ($18.2% per-share book value increase)
- **11 consecutive years of underwriting profit** ($22B cumulative pre-tax)
- **Powerhouse Five** (BNSF, MidAmerican, Iscar, Lubrizol, Marmon) delivered **$10.8B pre-tax earnings**
- **Insurance float grew to $77.2B** (vs. $41B in 2002)
- **GEICO passed Allstate** to become #2 U.S. auto insurer

### Core Insights

#### Intrinsic Value Growth
> "Berkshire's intrinsic value far exceeds its book value. The difference has widened considerably in recent years." [[p.2]]

- **Per-share investments**: $129,253 (+13.6% YoY)
- **Operating earnings**: $9,116/share (+12.8% YoY)
- **Book value growth**: 19.7% CAGR since 1965

#### Insurance Economics
> "Our revolving float is both costless and long-enduring... the true value of this liability is dramatically less than the accounting liability." [[p.7]]

| Segment | 2013 Underwriting Profit | 2013 Float |
|---------|--------------------------|------------|
| BH Reinsurance | $1,294M | $37,231M |
| General Re | $283M | $20,013M |
| GEICO | $1,127M | $12,566M |
| Other Primary | $385M | $7,430M |
| **Total** | **$3,089M** | **$77,240M** |

#### Powerhouse Five
> "The $10.4B gain in annual earnings delivered by the five companies over nine years has been accompanied by only minor dilution." [[p.3]]

- **BNSF**: 15% of U.S. inter-city freight (500 ton-miles/gallon efficiency)
- **MidAmerican**: 7% of U.S. wind capacity, #1 customer satisfaction (95.3%)
- **GEICO**: 2nd largest auto insurer (low-cost moat)
- **Marmon & Iscar**: Completed full acquisitions ($1.8B accounting anomaly)

#### Management Philosophy
> "We much prefer owning a non-controlling but substantial portion of a wonderful company to owning 100% of a so-so business." [[p.5]]

- **Big Four holdings**: American Express (14.2%), Coca-Cola (9.1%), IBM (6.3%), Wells Fargo (9.2%)
- **Unrealized gains**: $39B across Big Four holdings
- **Repurchase discipline**: Only at ≤120% of book value

### 📌 Notable Quotes

> "America's best days lie ahead." [[p.6]]

> "The advantage of being bi-sexual is that it doubles your chances for a date on Saturday night. Similarly, our appetite for either operating businesses or passive investments doubles our chances of finding sensible uses for our endless gusher of cash." [[p.6]]

> "When Wall Streeters tout EBITDA as a valuation guide, button your wallet." [[p.13]]
